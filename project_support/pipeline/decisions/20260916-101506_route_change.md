- **时间**: 2026-09-16 10:15:06
- **类型**: route_change
- **问题**: Is OHRS (object-hypothesis referent supervision) admissible as the project's novel mechanism, given RefMask3D and the banned assignment-successor family?
- **决策**: Reject OHRS as the contribution and do not enqueue it;record that the diagnosed bottleneck has a known prior-art fix,so the remaining novelty space must be re-scoped
- **依据**: RefMask3D(arXiv2407.18244v1,ACMMM24)verified from the original PDF uses Hungarian matching with classification+BCE+dice cost then Lmatch=l_cls L_cls+l_bce L_bce+l_dice L_dice on the matched object,so mask-aware referent positives are established prior art and fall inside the banned assignment-successor family. Independently:linear readout on the query embedding+mask geometry+the model's own phrase-group language attention does not beat the deployed head(top1.110 vs.178),and query-vs-region pooling shows region features collapse same-name discriminability(cos.895 vs query.677),which rules out readout and region-scoring repairs.
