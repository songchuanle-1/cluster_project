2026-09-14. Candidate M195-CTF-HCG-CSORT-CPS, independent M95 branch.

## Decision And Boundary
Scientific mechanism accepted for one Phase1 structural first screen, subject
to final static audit, code synchronization and locked queue checks.
This is permission to test an explicitly unverified inductive bias, not proof
of efficacy or first-ever novelty. No constraint or banned item was relaxed.
The conditional-logistic primitive is standard. The experimental distinction
is four native classifier-logit cells on common physical target-owned seeds
across two native same-scene descriptions. Row and column additive biases
cancel exactly. It is not within-bank IoU ranking, a new embedding head,
language routing, or a successor to closed readback variants.

Original-paper boundaries are recorded in design1705: ConcreteNet Eq11,
CLIP Fig3, VSGM section3.4.2 Eq16 and Rank&Sort sections3/4.
Their stored full texts support those comparisons, not an exhaustive novelty
certificate. Additional web exact-application queries returned no usable
results. No first-ever claim is made; unknown overlapping publications remain
a limitation. Broad contrastive learning itself is not claimed as new.
