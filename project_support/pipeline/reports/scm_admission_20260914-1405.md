## Scientific Boundary

SCM constructs one predicted spatial probability measure from the native
selector probabilities and all candidate masks, then supervises its three
coordinate marginals against the existing target-mask measure. It is training
only and does not add an inference head, score fusion, or reranking stage.

Wasserstein supervision, projected/sliced transport, offset-head losses, and
generic mask-quality reranking are prior art. The admitted claim is therefore
an experimentally distinct application and optimization coupling, not invention
of transport mathematics and not exhaustive novelty certification.
