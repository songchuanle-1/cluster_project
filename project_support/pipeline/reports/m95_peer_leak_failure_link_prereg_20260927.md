superpoints and an annotated same-class peer physically present. Freeze all

superpoints between deployed failure and success, stratifying the original
fragmented, other-limited, covered-control, and remainder groups. Preserve
all denominators, scene counts, mask-limited versus mask-capable counts, and
identities. No posthoc threshold, exclusion, or model change.
