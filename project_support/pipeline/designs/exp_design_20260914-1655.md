## 3. Analysis And Direction
Use EXACT SCM reference formulation in scm_math_check_20260914.py.
Read frozen Sep12 M95 mask/indi logits, GT and SP membership. Recover raw
coordinates via per-SP mean after verifying np.unique(raw_SP) inverse mapping
equals saved point membership. No coordinate renormalization beyond SCM's D.
Hash every bank/raw scene and validate capture manifest hashes.
These are evaluation coordinates, not historical training augmentations.

Let d=indi1-indi0, s=argmax d, o=first argmax hard IoU, gradient g=dSCM/dd.
The instantaneous change in d_s-d_o under selector-logit gradient descent is
v=-g_s+g_o. On recoverable failures (selected IoU<=.5,oracle>.5):
v<0 favors oracle, v>0 favors selected, |v|<=1e-12 is numerical zero.
This is a logit-space SCM-only direction; not network/Adam/full-loss direction.
No finite update size is selected and no optimizer is run.
Capture FP16 quantization and argmax ties may affect the result.

Compute individual normalized-mask transport costs C_q. Compare C_s versus C_o
separately: individual-cost ordering is not the same as mixture-gradient ordering.
Also compute all-pair selector-only expected-hard-IoU directional derivative:
alpha=normalize(sigmoid(d)), J_q=hardIoU_q, E=sum alpha*J;
dE/dd_q=alpha_q*(J_q-E)*(1-sigmoid(d_q)); velocity=-dot(dE/dd,g).
Masks fixed: do not differentiate hard thresholds. This soft-expectation
quantity does not imply an infinitesimal argmax change.

Negative controls: success and coverage-limited strata reported in full.
Use finite difference on s and o in EVERY bank, query permutation invariance,
and exact hard IoU recalculation. Fail loudly on malformed/NaN/missing data.
Scene bootstrap10000 draws seed1999,95% percentile intervals for adverse
fraction and expected-hard-IoU decreasing fraction, reweight by banks per scene.
Report bank/scene denominators; mined validation evidence remains exploratory.
