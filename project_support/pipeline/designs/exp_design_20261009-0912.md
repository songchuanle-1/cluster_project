variants. Mean pooling destroys point detail; a constant SP logit cannot
represent19 mixed-boundary targets in the independently frozen128-row cohort.

CODEX_FORCE_DESIGN was scoped to begin, not saved as a policy change.

## 3. Analysis And Direction
Let s(p) be the unchanged SP index, f_p the original pre-pooling backbone
feature, d_p=f_p-mean_{p:s(p)=s}(f_p), q a final native query.
Solve for a fine logit field preserving each coarse SP logit:

    z_qp = z_q,s(p) + h(q)^T d_p
    h(q) = Linear_32(ReLU(Linear_256_to_32(q)))
    mean_{p in s}(z_qp) = z_qs

Width32 is the inherited backbone channel dimension. No searched bottleneck,
temperature, init, dropout, coefficient, schedule or selector parameter.
The mean identity is logit conservation, NOT probability/IoU conservation.
For a singleton or constant-feature SP the new component vanishes exactly.
Broadcasting f to its SP mean is the matched mechanism removal. An SP-constant
feature translation leaves the detail field invariant. Shared point/query
gradients are native; no claim of independent optimization or proven efficacy.

The final selected query is unchanged. Inference evaluates its point field
only, O(P*32+256*32); training evaluates the native matched queries, O(P*K*32),
retaining all original native losses/matches and adding point BCE/Dice with
the inherited mask coefficients. The point target is the existing native
gt_pmask; no pseudo label, anchor, new dataset or label repair.

## 7. Risk Assessment
Mixed boundaries are modest and not all errors; the mechanism may not close
the .0080 gap or may harm interior masks. Mean-logit conservation is not
thresholded-mask conservation. New supervision can change shared backbone.
Large matched-query point tensors can cost training memory; native measure
and bounded safety checks precede any launch.
Point refinement is known; limited original review supports a structural
difference from PointRend, not exhaustive novelty or a publishable claim.
