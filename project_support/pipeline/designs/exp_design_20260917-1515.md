## 3. Analysis & Direction
### 3.1 Confirmed defect
Holding the checkpoint fixed, restricting selection to native training positives
raises scene-balanced TRAIN success from 68.75% to 86.7%; the any-object control is
exactly null. Referent identity is the measured selection failure.

### 3.2 Rejected candidate
The candidate considered was paired factual/counterfactual evidence supervision:
remove or swap the referred object's evidence during training and require the matched
referent score to fall on the counterfactual.

### 3.3 Novelty decision
SwapMix 2204.02285v1 already swaps visual-context object features, uses the
counterfactual to diagnose reliance, and trains with the perturbation as a
regularizer. Project decision 20260916-024658 also records SwapMix context swapping
as prior art. Moving the operation from image objects to ScanRefer predicted masks
changes the application surface and intervention unit, not the core causal mechanism.

### 3.4 Resource consequence
The candidate needs an additional training view and increases peak activation memory.
Because the mechanism fails novelty first and offers no inference-time structural
change, it is not worth resource-gate development.
