### Candidate Derivation And Rejection
Let r be unobserved binary target-ownership, p=sigmoid(z) visual target match,
and pi=P(r=1|text). The obvious role-mixture likelihood is
L=-log(pi*p+(1-pi)*(1-p)).
Its logit derivative is
-(2*pi-1)*p*(1-p)/(pi*p+(1-pi)*(1-p)).
At pi=.5 the loss is log2 for every p, hence zero gradient. Optimizing a free
pi independently per example gives -log(max(p,1-p)), which encourages existing
certainty and accepts either role. Known pi in{0,1} reduces to ordinary BCE.
Analytic/finite-difference checks completed; no model training is warranted
for this unidentifiable formulation. It does not reject a genuinely identified
contextual role model. Ordinary BCE and latent mixtures are not new mechanisms.
