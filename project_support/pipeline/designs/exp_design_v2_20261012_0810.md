# Experiment Design: Target Mention Role, V2

## 1. Motivation
One native TRAIN case assigns target noun supervision to "window" while its
annotated target12 is "trash can". Test a structural positive-set correction,
not inference routing or an extra loss weight. M95 comparison0.5020;
ScanRefer goal0.5100. No trained improvement is known.

## 2. Core Mechanism And Derivation
Let S be the unique nonempty literal annotation-label span, disjoint from the
graphroot target spans. Native tokenizer supplies its normalized distribution p.
For the exact original annotated physical target IDs only:
P'=p; O'=clip(O+P,0,1)*(1-support(p)).
All other token maps, matching/query masks, original loss terms/weights stay fixed.
Original semantic alignment L(P',O') changes positive/negative noun roles;
its existing query/text projections and parameters receive native gradients.
No learned tensor is added. p and O' are supervision constants, not forward input.
Identity/multiple/absent/overlapping/wrong-ID cases bypass without new assignments.
Literal labels may still be linguistically ambiguous; eligibility is not proof
of physical co-reference. No category similarity invents identity.
Startup catalog scans unchanged TRAIN once; sparse batch rebinding is bounded
by native token maps. Predict never calls rebind (native control observed0 calls).

## 3. Comparison And Controls
M95 is the trained comparator; same-forward original maps isolate role correction
under identical candidate-local ordered numerical primitives. Identity delta0,
wrong-reference gradient separation, and literal-only anchoring increment are
recorded in root_role_incremental_20261012_0810.json.
Wrong annotated ID bypass is exact. Full versus empty TRAIN catalog predict
outputs identical across28 tensor fields on the fixed four-expression batch.
V2 batch1/2 strict1e-6 forward/loss/backward repeats pass. Original v1 FAILs
and both adapter failures remain archived, not reclassified.
Official BUTD-DETR already performs annotation-name positive-span anchoring:
that and original semantic alignment are not novel. Whole increment is
coupled old parsed target-to-other-role reassignment under unique disjoint-root
eligibility. Exhaustive novelty and population error prevalence remain unknown.

## 4. Evaluation And Rejection
Frozen original VAL overall ScanRefer mIoU plus scene-clustered uncertainty.
Predefined literal-outside-root, no-literal-match and unchanged subgroups;
literal eligibility is not an oracle identity annotation. With sufficient
scene evidence, material subgroup harm>=0.002 rejects the mechanism; insufficient
data is inconclusive. Original branch-stop and convergence definitions retained.
No test-set selection, new dataset, extra labels, additional seeds or tuning.
No fixed pretraining effect-size threshold is fabricated.

## 7. Risks And Unknowns
2115 TRAIN outside-root observations and1332 eligible catalog entries do not
certify error rates. Noun-role correction may be insufficient/harmful; modifiers/
relations/pronouns remain unchanged. Source novelty, trained mIoU, noise floor,
training efficiency and broad robustness remain unknown. Predicted timings
from partial original pretrain loading are execution resources, not efficacy.
