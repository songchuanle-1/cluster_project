## 3. Analysis & Direction
Positive support masses t_i,a_i and fixed physical heights z_i define
R=sum_ij t_i*a_j*1[z_i<z_j]/(T*A),T=sum t,A=sum a.
After stable height ordering,strict tied groups use cumulative anchor mass after
the entire group. R=sum_i t_i*A_above(z_i)/(T*A).
O(NlogN)sort,O(N)space;no NxN pointtensor in actual primitive.
dR/dt_i=(A_above(z_i)/A-R)/T.
dR/da_j=(T_below(z_j)/T-R)/A.
Mass gradients differentiable;coordinates treated fixed,not learned gradients
through changing order. Empty/zero-mass support connectedzero;invalidmassesreject.
R(t,a)+R(a,t)+P(ties)=1. Positivecoordinate affine and massscale invariant.
Equalcentroids can have differentR. No arbitrarytemperature or margin.
Rawpoint masks in audit are oracle diagnostic inputs,not deploymentfeatures.
No claim this statistic itself is novel or sufficient for relationmeaning.

### Unknown-Anchor Identifiability
With normalized target mass t and freely optimized anchor mass a,
R(t,a)=sum_j a_j*F_t(z_j), a convex combination of target cumulative masses.
Its unconstrained maximum over the simplex is max_j F_t(z_j);mass can move to
the highest admissible support irrespective of anchor noun. Thus geometric
satisfaction alone cannot identify a named referent. This is a limitation of
free-anchor integration,not a rejection of semantic-constrained relation models.
Required control before integration:hold targetmask/geometry fixed,replace only
anchor phrase with an unrelated existing noun, and test whether anchor support
and relation result respond. Geometric/extremum-only anchor is a negativecontrol.
A semantically responsive distribution is necessary,not sufficient for correctness
or novelty. No new annotation or automatic lexical role assignment authorized.
