## 3. Analysis And Direction
Freeze all284 M95 semantic-capture banks (71 scenes), source hashes, checkpoint,
sample indices and GT. Recompute labels using current `match_sampled_ins` and
raw scene superpoint majority labels, including nearest-query fallback.
Verify capture hashes, GT and forward logits; never reuse another capture's
query numbering. Masks/IoUs define diagnostic pairs only.

Let d_q=z_q1-z_q0; y_q is actual seed assignment; P=sum y; w_q=1 if y_q
else source eos_coef=.1. Source final-layer weighted CE has gradient
c_q=w_q(sigmoid(d_q)-y_q)/P.
For fixed selected s and highest-scored usable positive h, m=d_s-d_h.
At frozen head inputs u and actual head parameters phi:

    a = grad_phi m
    g = sum_q c_q grad_phi d_q
    v_head = -a dot g
    v_head = v_pair + v_other_positive + v_other_negative
    v_independent_two_logits = -2(c_s-c_h)

No Q-by-Q or N-by-N matrix is built. Autograd uses several vector-Jacobian
products over the small head. Native source loss value/gradient must match
the independently reconstructed CE up to its constant target entropy.
The configured outer positive loss coefficient is reported separately;
it scales all head velocities but cannot change their signs.

Conditional candidate control, NOT an admitted mechanism:
minimize .5||delta+g||^2 subject to a dot delta<=0.
If a dot(-g)>0 and ||a||>0, delta=-g-
(a dot(-g)/||a||^2)*a; otherwise delta=-g.
This protects only one frozen diagnostic margin to first order, not an
end-to-end training guarantee. Standard gradient-projection prior art may
fully subsume it. Do not deploy oracle pair selection.
