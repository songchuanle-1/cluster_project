## 3. Analysis & Direction
For horizontal anchor points x, define c=mean(x), covariance C, eigenvectors V,
u=V^T(x-c), bounds l=min(u),h=max(u). Axis1 has larger eigenvalue.
For target center t let v=V^T(t-c).
Distance to a finite short face at y in {l1,h1} is
sqrt((v0-clip(v0,l0,h0))^2+(v1-y)^2). Take the lesser of the two.
Exchange axes for long faces. No all-point NxN matrix is formed.
This diagnostic has no learned loss or differentiability claim. Covariance,
rectangles and geometric encodings are standard, not a contribution.
