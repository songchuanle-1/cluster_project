## 3. Analysis & Direction
  ### 3.1 Effective directions
    - Multi-scale context readback remains the confirmed +0.0118 core mechanism.
    - Shuffled recurrent context reaches +0.0086 over baseline, indicating a useful
      scene/distribution component.
  ### 3.2 Failed directions
    - Semantic foreground retrieval, foreground/background contrast, reciprocal
      query-to-point feedback, and MLay seed replication do not break the plateau.
  ### 3.3 Unablated directions
    - Direct subtraction of rival-query context.
    - Stable support intersection across decoder layers.
    - Point-level disagreement with fine-cluster occupancy.
  ### 3.4 Conclusion
    - Add three mutually exclusive structural variants to restore queue reserve and
      close these remaining causal gaps without tuning.
