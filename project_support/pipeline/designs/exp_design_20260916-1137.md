## 3. Analysis & Direction
### 3.1 Established this tick
A scene-grouped nonlinear head over the head's own input (plus position and mask
geometry it discards) reaches only 59.3% / 62.6% on the accept / positive labels,
against the deployed head's 69.1% / 68.3% and the 87.0% oracle positive restriction.
Readout engineering cannot deliver the measured gap.

### 3.2 The complete chain
1. Coverage: 118/123 expressions have at least one acceptable query; mean 26.3.
2. Defect: the deployed score prefers an unacceptable query over ~26 acceptable ones
   in 27.3% of expressions (scene-balanced, CI [18.0, 37.5]).
3. Causality: restricting to the native positive set fixes 22 and breaks 0, +17.9 pp
   (CI [+10.9, +25.8]); restricting to objectness alone is exactly null.
4. Feasibility: a scene-generalising nonlinear readout of the head's own input cannot
   approach that ceiling, so the fix must change what the model learns.
5. Novelty: the indicated learned change (referent positives agreeing with the object
   hypothesis) is RefMask3D mask-aware matching / DETR one-to-one assignment, i.e.
   prior art, and is not this project's contribution.

### 3.3 Direction
No admission. The one axis where a contribution is still needed - a learned mechanism
that supplies referent identity to the decision without being prior-art matching - has
no derived candidate at this tick. The remaining decision is the scope question
(101512), and the slots stay empty by the goal's own rule.
