### 3.3 Offline Falsifier
Use three superpoints at x=(0,1,2), target=(1,0,0), two query masks:

    m_bad=(.49,.51,.001); m_good=(.51,.49,.49)
    b_q=m_q/sum(m_q); alpha=normalize(sigmoid(score))
    p=sum_q alpha_q*b_q; L=SCM(p,target)

Under strict probability threshold .5, their hard IoUs are0 and1. Nevertheless
normalized soft mass can make bad's transport smaller. At equal selector logits,
the analytical reference gradient can then favor bad. Check finite differences,
query permutation, and a small selector-only gradient step.
These constants define a deterministic counterexample, not training hyperparameters.
This is not the equal-coordinate-marginal degeneracy: positions are distinct
and each candidate has nonzero transport. BCE/CE/auxiliary losses are excluded;
therefore the example does NOT predict the sign of full training gradients.
Complexity O(QN + 3N log N); no GPU, new data or checkpoint access.
