decision to be about the object hypothesis, (b) is not RefMask3D/DETR matching, (c)
is not in the 11 goal.json bans, and (d) has a derivation, matched controls and
pre-defined rejection criteria. None is available at this tick, so the slots stay
empty by the goal's own rule.
