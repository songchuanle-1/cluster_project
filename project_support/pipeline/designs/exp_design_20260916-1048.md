## 3. Analysis & Direction
### 3.1 The full evidence chain now
1. Coverage: 33 of 38 fixed-forward failures already contain a query above IoU .5
   with the target; only 5 are mask-limited; sampling loses 0 of 117 covered targets.
2. Error type: all 33 wrong selections cleanly segment a different instance
   (median IoU .864, 11 perfect, 28 with zero overlap with the target).
3. Supervision: the head tracks seed containment, not its own mask. Mean indis is
   .047 (seed-/mask-), .130 (seed-/mask+), .848 (seed+/mask-), .876 (seed+/mask+).
4. Readouts: 23 scoring variants plus two probes all fail to rank the correct query.
5. Representation (new): centred instance feature similarity is .270 between target
   and the wrongly chosen instance versus a .029 null, and the target is the nearest
   neighbour of the wrong instance in 21.4% of cases (chance 3.3%) — but is outside
   the top-5 in 46% of cases. The features encode category, not identity.
6. Prior art: the standard supervision-side fix is RefMask3D's Hungarian matching
   with cls+BCE+dice cost, and is banned here as an assignment successor.

### 3.2 Direction
The required information is relational/spatial identity evidence among same-name
instances at ~1.3 m separation. It has to be introduced, not re-read. The remaining
design space must simultaneously:
- add a new information flow (not a scorer, gate, pooling, selector, re-weighting,
  calibration, contrastive, competition, rerank, mask-aware matching or assignment);
- stay inside the ~0.24 G FLOPs headroom and the no-N^2 rule;
- be argued against NS3D (compositional programs / relations), JVGN (phrase graph
  marginalisation), RefMask3D (linguistic primitives + object cluster), InstAlign
  and HCF-RES.

### 3.3 Explicit anti-claims recorded
- Do not cite "the encoder cannot separate instances at all": it does, 9x above null.
- Do not cite "region pooling destroyed the signal": the features are
  category-dominated by construction; pooling inherits that.
- Do not claim the readout probe bounds training changes; it bounds readouts only.
