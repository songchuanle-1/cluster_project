## 3. Analysis & Direction
All per-expression SCM equations, gradients, degeneracies and evaluation
preregistration in designs/exp_design_20260914-0945.md remain, except reduction:

    D_e = (1/K_e) sum_(matched q) Dice(q,target)
    parent_rank = sum_e D_e / K_global
    SCM_rank = sum_(valid e) T_e / max(K_global,1)
    K_global = sum_r sum_e K_re
    V_global = sum_r sum_e valid_re

V_global is reporting/exclusion evidence, not a replacement denominator.
Equivalently SCM_rank = (V_global/K_global) *
(local transport sum/V_global) when V_global>0; this factor is an identity,
not an estimated coefficient. Keep weight1. DDP averages rank gradients:
both parent and SCM therefore carry the inherited 1/world_size relative to a
single-process concatenated numerator/global denominator. Do NOT silently
multiply SCM alone by world_size. Explicit tests assert this inherited scaling.
When all SCM expressions are invalid the new term is connected zero. The
unchanged parent's other losses may still reject all-empty target batches.

Nearest original-source equations:
- Learning with a Wasserstein Loss, arXiv1506.05439v3, Eq(3):
  min_(T in Pi(h(x),y)) <T,M>, M_ij=d(i,j)^p. SCM uses output measures too;
  normalized score-mask mixture is its h, but only three marginal W1 costs,
  not the general KxK transport plan or entropic Sinkhorn solver.
- Generalized Sliced Wasserstein Distances, arXiv1902.00434v1,
  Eq(3): Wp=(integral_0^1 |F_mu^-1(z)-F_nu^-1(z)|^p dz)^(1/p);
  Eq(7): SWp=(integral_sphere Wp^p(Rmu,Rnu)dtheta)^(1/p).
  SCM fixes p=1 and three coordinate projections; prefix imbalance times
  interval length is the discrete CDF form. It is a pseudometric, not their
  full-sphere metric or generalized nonlinear/maximized projector.
- Fine-Grained Spatial and Verbal Losses, arXiv2411.03405, Eq(4):
  L_sel=H(u,u_hat); Eq(5): L_o=sum_i L2(o_i,c_i-c_gt);
  Eq(6): L_sp=H1(l,l_hat). SCM has no offset/span head or centroid regression;
  it changes final Dice and differentiates existing masks AND selection scores.
These exact differences from retained originals do not establish absence of
closer mixture-output grounded segmentation work. Application novelty OPEN.
