## 3. Analysis And Direction
For n>0 boundary logits z, binary labels y and k=sum(y), define
Z_k=sum_{|S|=k} exp(sum_{i in S} z_i).
L_count=(sum_i softplus(z_i)-log Z_k)/n.
Let q_i=P(i in S | |S|=k,z), computed by exact subset enumeration in this audit.
Then dL_count/dz_i=(sigmoid(z_i)-q_i)/n.
Both loss and gradient depend on y only through k. Swapping foreground and
background labels at fixed k leaves them unchanged. Thus no assignment identity
can be recovered from this objective alone.
For z=(a,-a), a>0, k=1: q_1=sigmoid(2a)>sigmoid(a).
Descent increases z_1 and decreases z_2. For y=(0,1), this reinforces the wrong
assignment; for y=(1,0), it reinforces the correct one, with identical loss.
At z=(0,0), k=1, the gradient is zero regardless of target identity.
This refutes a guaranteed corrective assignment mechanism, not all possible
count-calibration benefits or behavior under the complete retained model loss.
Complexity: exhaustive audit O(n*2^n); deployable DP would be O(n*min(k,n-k)).
No production DP or native resource claim is made.
