## 3. Analysis & Direction
### 3.1 What is confirmed
- Deployed selection failures are real on the bounded same-forward capture.
- Every one of the 8 failures has an acceptable candidate query in the same
  forward.
- Positive-only selection is a strong oracle repair, but is not deployable.

### 3.2 What is refuted or unresolved
- Reject the causal claim that the repair is referent-specific on this capture:
  the any-seed control also repairs nearly all failures.
- Reject candidate coverage as the current explanation: 0/16 expressions lack
  an acceptable query.
- Leave same-class discrimination unresolved: only 2/8 failures are known
  same-class; 6 target semantic labels are -100.
- Reject immediate queueing of ranking, mask-aware matching, relation transport,
  identity fusion, or renamed modality variants; each is already closed or prior
  art under the current scope.

### 3.3 Proposed diagnostic sequence
1. Recover exact semantic instance identities for the six unresolved failures
   from the native ScanRefer/ScanNet metadata without GPU work.
2. Pre-register a larger same-forward manifest with complete semantics, explicit
   target/wrong/acceptable-query controls, and no fallback selection rule.
3. Re-evaluate identity preservation and referent-versus-objectness separation.
4. Only after that evidence, derive a candidate mechanism and run the full
   motivation review, novelty check, smell check, native backward, resource and
   independence gates.

### 3.4 Decision
Admit no training variant and do not mutate the queue.
