### 3.3 Mechanism
For valid query q and superpoint n:

    alpha_q = softmax_q(logsigmoid(indi_q,1 - indi_q,0))
    b_qn = softmax_n(logsigmoid(mask_qn))
    p_n = sum_q alpha_q b_qn
    t_n = target_n / sum_j target_j

For each physical coordinate axis, sort valid superpoints and compute the
interval-weighted absolute cumulative mass mismatch. Average the three 1D
transport costs and normalize coordinate gaps by the scene diagonal.

    L_SCM = mean_axis sum_k normalized_gap_k * abs(cumsum_k(p - t))

Replace final Dice only:

    L_new = L_M95 - L_final_dice + L_SCM

No parameter, temperature, fitted coefficient, top-k rule, oracle candidate
identity, new inference head, or test-time reranking is introduced.

### 3.4 Prior-art boundary
  - Metric-aware Wasserstein losses and projected/sliced Wasserstein distances
    establish the transport mathematics as prior art.
  - FGS/AsphaltNet establishes offset-head spatial regression as prior art.
  - Mask Scoring R-CNN and ranking losses establish generic mask-quality scoring
    and reranking as prior art.
  - SCM is admitted as an experimentally distinct training construction: native
    selector probabilities and all candidate masks define one spatial measure,
    supervised against target-mask marginals, while inference remains unchanged.
  - No exhaustive novelty certification or speedup claim is made.

## 7. Risk Assessment
  - Scientific: the soft mixture may improve while hard selection does not.
  - Mathematical: coordinate marginals can miss different joint 3D supports.
  - Optimization: normalized masks discard absolute area; inherited BCE and
    auxiliary Dice remain to constrain area and segmentation.
  - Causal: historical M95 is not a new matched training rerun.
  - Resource: inference graph is unchanged, but timing noise is measurable.
  - Failure fallback: close SCM at <+0.002 after plateau; do not rescue it with
    coefficient, seed, temperature, or direction sweeps.
