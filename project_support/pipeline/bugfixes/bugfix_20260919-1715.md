# Bugfix: native query seed axis and expression identity

- Time: 2026-09-19, event tick generated at 17:07 Asia/Shanghai.
- Experiments: seven due frozen-M95 diagnostic stages; not a training crash.
- Error type: silent diagnostic object-assignment error and lossy identity coercion.
- Reproduction:
  - Native FPS [4,0,2], sampled query indices [0,1,2] incorrectly projected
    to [0,1,2] rather than [4,0,2].
  - ann_id=10.9 and dataset_index=False were silently accepted for IDs 10 and 0.
  - The expanded producer suite ran 17 tests before the fix and reported
    47 failing assertions/subtests, including the FPS mapping regression.
- Root cause: the adapter treated sample_inds as native superpoint indices.
  The frozen producer explicitly computes held["fps"][b][pred["sample_inds"][b]]
  and separately serializes fps_superpoints and sampled_seed_indices.
  Physical-key construction also used unrestricted int() conversion.
- Fix:
  - Require the native FPS axis and validate its support bounds and integer types.
  - Validate sampled indices on the FPS axis and compose FPS[sample_inds].
  - Accept only nonnegative integers or canonical decimal strings for identity;
    reject booleans, floats, negative values, whitespace and padded aliases.
  - Exercise non-identity FPS permutations in the integrated synthetic fixture.
- Verification: 65 focused tests pass; all six updated chain checks pass.
- Retry: first targeted fix, statically verified. No native GPU execution.
- Scope: CPU diagnostic adapter and tests only; frozen seven-file producer,
  model, config, queue, checkpoint and running M197-PIRS are unchanged.
- Evidence limitation: the earlier identity-FPS synthetic self-test did not
  establish correctness under general FPS ordering. Its artifact is retained,
  not rewritten; the new self-test supersedes its seed-projection assurance.
- Scientific effect remains unknown until native capture. No admission,
  efficacy, novel-mechanism or population-effect claim follows from this fix.
